# Color

Use `Color.*`; `Core.Color.*` only supports aliases. Use `Color.Text.*`, `Surface.*`, `Border.*`, `Action.*`, and `Status.*` by role. Sidebar, overlay, skeleton, avatar, icon, and accent roles stay in their contexts. Pair status color with text/icon/shape and confirm meaning in Light/Dark.

Never add raw hex/RGB/HSL, named colors, or framework palette classes to product components. Propose a missing semantic role with all required modes.
