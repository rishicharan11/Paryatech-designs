# Design decision tests

Choose the option that reveals what changed, who owns it, and what happens next; reuses known patterns; remains legible at realistic data density; preserves meaning across themes/densities; separates internal and customer information; and works without color, hover, or perfect data.

Lead pages with purpose and primary action. Put summaries before detail, exceptions before passive history, and current work before archives. A dashboard metric must lead to relevant records or explain a decision.

A new pattern requires a repeated need, no suitable existing variant/composition, defined content/interaction rules, complete states, and an owner.

Agent outputs should state workflow/role, reused components/tokens, state/responsive coverage, accessibility, and explicit gaps.
